## So I chose to run away

### Merch hunting in Beijing

On a rare weekend I got up at 7:30 and boarded the train to Beijing. From Beijing South Railway Station I took metro Line 14 to Jiulongshan Station, and the moment I stepped out I was at my destination: **Chaoyang Hopson One**. I was immediately drawn in by the dazzling row of merch stores and had no choice but to go in and feast on a spree. I didn't expect them to have K-ON! merch too — now there was no escaping it (K-ON!). Below are the merch photos. It wasn't only merch stores either; there were board game and handmade shops too, but since I was short on time I didn't go in and try them.

I'd originally arranged to go out with a friend, but plans changed at the last minute, so I soloed the merch stores. I got a blind-box can badge from Bocchi the Rock (first pull landed Bocchi), and also bought 罗 mio and 朱律叶!

P.S.: There are also plenty of light novels, so if you're interested you can buy them there in the future. I was really surprised that Beijing has so many merch stores, though according to my Beijing classmates there weren't actually many before, and I just happened to catch it.

![KON-goods](https://img.eurekaimer.icu/objects/default/4de312dd-6fa3-4f4e-9a5c-508900a34b0d.webp)

After browsing the merch stores for a while I got hungry, so I picked a random shop and filled my stomach. Just then I met up with a member of an Arknights group chat and we switched to two-player mode.

### GBC!

Then came the biggest destination of this flash trip: the GBC pop-up store. It had looked rather small from the outside, but there was quite a lot inside (photo warning ahead).

First, pictures of my favorite, Nina. Outside the venue there was a Beijing pop-up city-exclusive photo spot, and there was also a screening room playing the band's greeting video and songs (老冰 really slaps!). I stayed there quite a while and watched the video twice! There were also many GBC original art collections, but since they're original works photography wasn't allowed (I'm a good kid!). That picture of Nina by the lake is really beautiful, isn't it!

### Arknights

When I finished browsing it was still early, so on my friend's suggestion I took the metro to the Arknights pop-up store. The moment I stepped in I was startled: it was almost all girls, and they had queued in a huge loop that completely surrounded the Arknights pop-up. On one hand I marveled at how famous Arknights is; on the other I was astonished that, as expected of the capital, it could gather so many cosplayers, Lolita girls, and JK girls — almost as many as I've seen in two years of living in Tianjin.

But when I reached the entrance I found they weren't queueing for Arknights at all, but for Qi Yu from Love and Deepspace (the power of otome games is terrifying). The Arknights store wasn't especially crowded, and the merch was almost all practical stuff (bath towels, notebooks, bags). I wasn't very interested in the characters in this collab, so I just looked around.

Because I'd been walking around for most of the day I was really tired, so I found a dessert shop and ordered a matcha parfait (doge).

![Matcha parfait](https://img.eurekaimer.icu/objects/default/4a19cbeb-37a1-4e94-ab9f-6a2143b35dfd.webp)

I gave my friend a crash course on Ave Mujica (this time collaborating with Arknights — 海猫络合物, five characters, a certified unhinged genius), and she was instantly stunned by the unhinged plot, saying she was really looking forward to the collab and the collab characters. I'd originally wanted to visit the A Café as well, but it was too far and I didn't have enough time, so I gave it up and got on the road back to Tianjin. Overall it was a lot of fun (and meeting the group chat person in real life and chatting was great too).
