>My earlier yuri categorization was too slapdash and I couldn't evaluate things properly, so I've split off a separate yuri reading list to roughly record the novels I've read and thought were pretty good

>Ongoing, updated roughly every half year (or whenever I remember while updating something else)

## Preface

Because I've read a lot of good yuri recently, I wanted a chance to take stock and record it — both to share my collection and recommendations with other friends, and to keep from forgetting some very interesting stories and characters because I've read too much (I often find that some plot points from certain books come back to me at some strange moment, but I've forgotten the title, or some books I simply can't find anymore)

>The sections below are divided by platform, but as everyone knows, platforms don't allow certain explicit content to be posted. I mean Jinjiang; Changpei isn't much better either. So once you know a title, it's best to go find the author's Weibo or some third-party site to read it, so you don't finish a whole watered-down version before realizing (darling, that's not friendship~) — why is what you're reading different from everyone else!
