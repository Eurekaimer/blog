# Anime Rank

> The masterpiece in your eyes isn't necessarily a masterpiece in someone else's, or even a standout work; likewise, a work others tear into might be the masterpiece in your heart. That is the first principle of any ACG ranking.
>
> The yuri here covers both light yuri and heavy yuri. The subject matter itself and the era it comes from both affect how a work is positioned, so this tag is mostly for filing and sorting, not the only way to read a work.

## Masterpiece

- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 超时空辉夜姬: the plot may have its flaws, but the animation and effects have broken through everything — the most lavishly produced battle a yuri show has ever had
- [x] 薰香花朵凛然绽放 (The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity): this one is also about catching the right wavelength; I really like the heroine (she's a bit like Chitanda), and the metaphors and hints the camera gives are actually quite well placed. I like it a lot
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> Girls Band Cry: the true GOAT of recent girls-band works in my heart. A voice crying out against fate, genuinely real conflicts and oppositions, a style that lands right in my strike zone, and extremely powerful 3D and animation
- [x] 葬送的芙莉莲 (Frieren: Beyond Journey's End): a work that builds emotion through plain storytelling — clues laid down faintly, a vein running a thousand li
- [x] 紫罗兰永恒花园 (Violet Evergarden): the work that got me in, one I will never forget. Extremely fine animation, pure feeling
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 终将成为你 (Bloom Into You): great yuri, great animation, great music, great detail — the most stunning hardcore yuri
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 白箱 (Shirobako): a different entry point, showing in detail the work and daily life of people in the anime industry, with real emotion inside the ordinary, and some historical narrative woven in too. An excellent work
- [x] 冰菓 (Hyouka): a light yet not light school mystery anime, which suits my personality (though a lot of people find it boring, in which case I think you should consider whether you're on its wavelength before watching)
- [x] 轻音少女 (K-On!): a groundbreaking work that opened the moe era. I want a third season (2025.9.14). I want a third season so badly!!!!!!!!
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 莉兹与青鸟 (Liz and the Blue Bird): the light and dark lines complete each other, music and staging are both mythical. Both of them Liz, each the other's blue bird. An exquisite, fresh fairy tale, and Naoko Yamada's peak
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 轻音少女 剧场版 (K-On! The Movie): no need to say more — K-ON is great. Even as a 2011 work it still feels full of sincerity

## Excellent

- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 我们不可能成为恋人！（※似乎可行？）: the yuri harem premise that inexplicably blew up in 2025; it feels a bit baffling to me. The original novels and manga are both fairly good, and the anime more or less keeps up a certain level of animation
- [x] 坏女孩 (Bad Girl): I like it quite a bit, a fairly light slice-of-life comedy
- [x] 孤独摇滚 (Bocchi the Rock!): came for the reputation, maybe it didn't reach my expectations. I'm socially anxious myself, but I didn't resonate much. It really is a very good work
- [x] 败犬女主太多了 (Makeine: Too Many Losing Heroines!): my favorite character Komari Chika is in it, but the limits of the premise overall probably keep it from masterpiece potential
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 樱 Trick (Sakura Trick): the kiss kiss of it all
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> citrus～柑橘味香气～ (Citrus): where the citrus flavor comes from, though the anime's execution may not be as good as the manga's
- [x] BanG Dream! It's MyGO!!!!!: an unexpected success? It opened up a new perspective on girls' bands. Personally I like the characters more than the show itself
- [x] 干物妹！小埋 (Himouto! Umaru-chan): it has a character I like (Kirie). The overall pacing leans light, and in that lightness few can match K-ON
- [x] 擅长逃跑的殿下 (The Elusive Samurai): I really like the art style and story — that's personal preference
- [x] 玉子市场 (Tamako Market)
- [x] 间谍过家家 1 (Spy×Family 1): a fairly warm work, excellent animation, voice acting, and story
- [x] 间谍过家家 2 (Spy×Family 2): a fairly warm work, excellent animation, voice acting, and story
- [x] 我的青春恋爱物语果然有问题 (My Youth Romantic Comedy Is Wrong, As I Expected): my youth romantic comedy has problems. I like Yui and Haruno, but I don't resonate much with the "genuine thing" and the riddles
- [x] 四月是你的谎言 (Your Lie in April): everyone says it's a knife to the heart, but I didn't feel much — maybe I watched it too late, and I'm used to taking knives
- [x] 石纪元 (Dr. Stone): more of a science-popularization anime, but a bit of a power fantasy
- [x] 魔女之旅 (Wandering Witch: The Journey of Elaina): I like Elaina, white-haired loli ( ). There are excellent parts, but I still want to keep the masterpiece count under control
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 转生王女与天才千金的魔法革命 (The Magical Revolution of the Reincarnated Princess and the Genius Young Lady): the ending is a bit open, and some parts in the middle didn't fully satisfy me, but it's still a rare yuri work
- [x] Charlotte: if not for the very abrupt ending, the stretch where he sinks into depravity alone would have had masterpiece strength
- [x] 擅长捉弄的高木同学 (Teasing Master Takagi-san): a super sweet one
- [x] 堀与宫村 (Horimiya): very sweet, very sour; the subject has a homogenization problem
- [x] 秒速五厘米 (5 Centimeters per Second): full of regret, full of pain
- [x] 天气之子 (Weathering with You)
- [x] 你的名字 (Your Name): too famous, but I don't feel much for this kind of subject anymore
- [x] 可塑性记忆 (Plastic Memories): the ending comes too fast, but the earlier part is excellent. I automatically give extra points to a female character with Isla's personality. If only there were a sequel!!!
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 摇曳百合 (Yuru Yuri): a very classic work — basically four people, two couples. There's a gloomy ghost senpai in it I quite like
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 摇曳百合♪♪ (Yuru Yuri♪♪): S2
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 摇曳百合 3☆High! (Yuru Yuri San☆Hai!): S3
- [x] <img class="yuri-icon" src="/blog/assets/icons/yuri-lily.svg" alt="Yuri" title="Yuri" /> 紫罗兰永恒花园 外传 - 永远与自动手记人偶 (Violet Evergarden: Eternity and the Auto Memory Doll): staging that seems to want to stir something up; many parts, seen on their own, feel like a lesbian film, but afterwards, emm — I suggest watching the whole thing (no spoilers from me)
