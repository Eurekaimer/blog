# About me

> I’ve wrestled with myself long enough. I’d rather be myself.

I’m Zhige (Eurekaimer), a statistics undergrad headed to the University of Science and Technology of China to keep studying probability and statistics. I spent the first half of college buried in measure theory, real analysis, and probability limit theorems, thinking I’d stay in academia. Doing research and seeing papers written to hit targets made me want to go back to my own desk. These days I’d rather open the book, work through the math, and run the code myself.

## How I ended up on Linux

In high school, I got my first taste of Linux running Minecraft and Terraria servers with friends. In my third year of university, I put Ubuntu on an external SSD to reproduce a paper. That was it for me. I fell for NixOS’s rollbacks, then moved to CachyOS when the FHS compatibility headaches got old. Now I use Niri, Neovim, Kitty, Ghostty, and a bunch of TUI tools. My favorite part of open source is getting to pick up where someone else left off, then sending my changes back.

## The yuri I keep coming back to

Nina Iseri giving the world the finger in *Girls Band Cry* stuck with me. So did the way Naoko Yamada makes letting go feel quiet in *Liz and the Blue Bird*. *Bloom Into You* gets at how hard it can be to accept yourself. I also read 七小皇叔, 扶华, and 闵然 on Changpei and Jinjiang.

## What I write here

I’ve used MkDocs and tried Zensical. The blog now runs on Astro. I write about yuri, keyboard workflows, what breaks when I code with language models, and things from college I’m still making sense of. I want to take my time with it without a feed picking the topics for me.

My probability, statistics, deep learning, and algorithms notes live on my [notes site](https://www.eurekaimer.icu/notes). Shorter thoughts go in [Moments](/blog/moments/), and my watch and play histories are on [Bangumi](/blog/bangumi/) and [Steam](/blog/steam/).
